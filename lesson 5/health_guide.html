💪 Why your body needs exercise

Your body is like a suparcar — the more you use it, the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes every dayare happier, sleep better and do better in school.

Dancing, swimming, football and yoga — anything that gets your heart beating faster. Keep moving and have fun!

☀ Quick Health Tips

  • 🍎 Eat 5 fruits and veggies every day
  • 💧 Drink 8 glasses of water daily
  • 😴 Sleep 9 to 11 hours every night
  • 🏃 Move your body for 60 minutes
